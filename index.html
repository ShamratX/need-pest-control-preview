Through the year

What Kitchener homes deal with each season

Typical patterns across southern Ontario.

  1. March to May

    Spring

    Raccoon and squirrel mothers look for warm, sheltered places to have their young. Attics and chimneys qualify.

  2. June to August

    Summer

    Wasp nests grow fast and are biggest by late summer. Skunks raise their young under decks and sheds. Bat pups are in attics from mid-May to mid-August, so openings stay as they are until the timing is right.

  3. September to November

    Fall

    Mice and rats move indoors as the nights turn cold. Squirrels look for a winter spot in the attic.

  4. December to February

    Winter

    Animals that got in during the fall stay put. Noises in the ceiling on cold nights are worth checking.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Something else on your mind? Call (226) 600-5597.

Which animals and pests do you deal with?

Raccoons, squirrels, skunks, bats, rats and mice, plus wasps, bees, spiders and other insects.

Do you repair the damage animals leave behind?

Yes. We replace damaged attic insulation and seal the entry points the animals used, so the next one can’t get in the same way.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. If you hear or see signs of animals again during the guarantee period, call and we’ll come back to check. Ask us for the guarantee details when you book.

What area do you serve?

We’re based in Kitchener and work on Kitchener homes. If you’re nearby, call and we’ll tell you whether we can get to you.

How can I tell what’s in my attic?

Timing helps. Scratching early in the morning usually points to squirrels. Heavier thumping at night is more often raccoons. Tell us when you hear it and we’ll know what to look for. Our attic noises guide has a quick comparison.
