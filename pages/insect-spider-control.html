Insect and spider control in Kitchener

Spiders or insects inside the house or along the outside walls? We treat the problem areas inside and around your home.

Spiders and other insects tend to collect where the house meets the ground: basements, window frames, door thresholds and the outside walls.

We treat the problem areas inside and around the house.

Signs you have insects and spiders

  • Spiders and webs collecting in corners, windows or the basement
  • Insects coming in around doors and windows
  • Activity along the foundation and outside walls

How we handle it

  1. InspectWe find where the insects are coming from.
  2. TreatProblem areas inside and around the house are treated.
  3. AdviseWe point out gaps worth sealing to keep them out.

Why spiders and insects collect where they do

Spiders follow their food. A basement window with a dim light, a door threshold that doesn’t seal or a damp corner draws insects, and the spiders arrive after them. The outside walls and the base of the house are where most problems begin, because that is where insects find gaps to get in.

Most spiders found in Ontario homes are harmless to people, but few people want them in the house. The most effective plan deals with the reason they are there and not only the spiders you can see.

What actually helps

Treating without fixing the cause means the problem returns. A lasting plan combines treatment of the problem areas with these steps:

  • Seal gaps around windows, doors, pipes and the foundation.
  • Fit door sweeps and repair damaged window screens.
  • Reduce clutter in basements, garages and closets where spiders hide.
  • Fix damp areas and use a dehumidifier in damp basements.
  • Switch outdoor lights to warm bulbs or move them away from doors, since lights draw insects.

What we need from you

Tell us what you are seeing, where it is in the house and when you first noticed it. If you can catch one in a jar or take a clear photo, bring it to the visit and we can tell you what it is and where it is coming from. If you are also hearing noises in the walls or attic, say so; it may be a mouse or rat problem and not an insect one.

Questions about insects and spiders

Do you handle bats as well?

Yes. Bat control is one of our services; call and tell us where you’re seeing them.

Which insects do you deal with?

Spiders and the other insects that come into homes. Call and describe what you’re seeing.

Are the spiders in my house dangerous?

Most spiders found in Ontario homes are harmless to people. If you are unsure, tell us what you are seeing or bring a photo, and we can tell you what it is.

Why do I keep seeing spiders after cleaning?

Spiders follow insects, and insects follow light, damp and gaps. Treating the spiders without fixing why they are there means they return.

Will treatment affect my pets or children?

Ask us before the visit and tell us who and what is in the house. We’ll explain what is used, where it is applied and how long to stay away from the area.
