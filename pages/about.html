About Need Pest Control

A Kitchener crew that gets animals out of houses and fixes what they broke.

What we do

Removal and repair, done in one job

We remove raccoons, squirrels, skunks, bats, mice and rats, wasp and bee nests, spiders and other insects from Kitchener homes.

Most calls start with a sound in the ceiling or a nest by the door. We find out what’s living there and how it got in, remove it, replace the insulation it ruined and seal the opening. Then we stand behind the work: if animals come back during the guarantee period, we come back too.

See what each job involves: raccoon removal, squirrel removal, skunk removal, bat control, mouse and rat control, wasp and bee nest removal, insect and spider control and animal damage repair. Not sure what you have? Try the attic noises guide or get in touch.

How we work

From the first call to sealed up

  1. You call

    Tell us what you’re hearing or seeing, where in the house and at what time of day.

  2. We inspect

    The outside of the house, the roofline and the attic, to find what’s there and how it gets in.

  3. We remove

    The animals or insects come out, along with the nest or den.

  4. We repair

    Damaged insulation is replaced and entry points are closed.

  5. We stand behind it

    If they come back during the guarantee period, call and we’ll return.
