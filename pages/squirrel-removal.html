Squirrel removal in Kitchener

Scratching in the ceiling early in the morning? We get squirrels out of attics and soffits, repair what they chewed and seal the gaps.

Squirrels chew. They open up soffits, fascia boards and roof vents to get into the attic, and once inside they can chew on wood and wiring.

We remove the squirrels, repair the chewed openings and close the gaps, so you stop hearing them at dawn.

Signs you have squirrels

  • Scratching and scurrying in the ceiling early in the morning or late afternoon
  • Chewed holes in soffits, fascia or roof vents
  • Squirrels going in and out at the roofline
  • Nesting material or droppings in the attic
  • Chewed insulation or wiring

How we handle it

  1. InspectWe find the openings at the roofline and check the attic for nests.
  2. RemoveThe squirrels and their nest come out.
  3. RepairChewed soffits, vents and damaged insulation are fixed.
  4. SealGaps are closed so they can’t chew back in at the same spot.

Squirrels are active by day, and they chew

Grey squirrels are the squirrels most Kitchener homeowners deal with. Unlike raccoons, they are awake in daylight, so scratching and running sounds that start at sunrise and fade by evening are a strong sign. They often get in at the roofline, through soffits, fascia boards, gable vents and the gaps where two roof sections meet.

Squirrels’ front teeth never stop growing, so they gnaw constantly to wear them down. In an attic that means wood, insulation and sometimes electrical wiring, which is the main reason to deal with them early.

Late winter, summer and fall are the busy times

Squirrels commonly raise young in late winter and again in summer, and many look for a warm place to spend the winter in fall. That is why calls about noise in the ceiling cluster in those months. If you hear faint chirping or squeaking along with the scratching, there may be young in the nest, which changes how and when the opening can be closed.

Why scaring them off rarely works

Ultrasonic devices, repellent sprays and a radio left on in the attic may move a squirrel for a night or two. Because the opening is still there, it usually comes back or another squirrel finds it. Trapping and driving a squirrel away isn’t a fix either: Ontario’s rules on wildlife that damage property say live-captured wildlife has to be released close to where it was caught.

The reliable approach is the one we follow: find every opening, remove the squirrels and their nest, repair what was chewed and seal the gaps with material they can’t gnaw through.

Reduce the odds of a repeat visit

A few habits make your roof a less attractive route:

  • Trim tree branches that reach or hang over the roof.
  • Keep bird feeders away from the house, since spilled seed draws squirrels close.
  • Fix soft or rotting wood on the fascia and soffits before squirrels find it.
  • Ask us to check the roofline once a year if you have had squirrels before.

Questions about squirrels

Why do I hear them in the morning?

Squirrels are active during the day, so scratching that starts at dawn and stops at night usually points to squirrels rather than raccoons.

When do squirrels move into attics?

Commonly in fall, when they look for a warm place for winter, and in spring, when they nest.

Do you repair the chewed holes?

Yes. Repairing and sealing the entry points is part of the job.

Can squirrels damage wiring?

Yes. Squirrels gnaw constantly, and in an attic that can include wiring. If you hear chewing, we check for wire damage and recommend an electrician if we see any.

Can I trap the squirrel myself?

Ontario rules require live-captured wildlife to be released near where it was caught, and removing the animal doesn’t close the opening. Without sealing the gap, another squirrel usually takes its place.

How do I know it’s squirrels and not mice?

Squirrels are heavier and louder, with running and rolling sounds in daylight. Mice make light scratching sounds, usually at night.
