By area of the house

Where animals and insects get into a house

Not sure what you have? Start with where you’re hearing or seeing it.

  • Attic and chimney

    Raccoons, squirrels and bats move into attics and chimneys. Noises overhead, droppings and flattened insulation are the usual signs.

    Raccoon removalSquirrel removalBat controlAnimal damage repair

  • Roof, soffits and vents

    Raccoons pull up shingles and vents, squirrels chew soffits, fascia and roof vents, and bats use small gaps along the roofline.

    Squirrel removalRaccoon removalBat controlAnimal damage repair

  • Decks, porches and sheds

    Skunks dig dens under decks, porches, sheds and steps. Most people notice the smell before they see one.

    Skunk removal

  • Eaves, siding and doors

    Wasps build nests under eaves, behind siding and around door frames, and bees swarm near walls and decks.

    Wasp and bee nest removal

  • Basement and foundation

    Mice fit through foundation cracks and gaps around pipes, and rats follow food and shelter into garages and basements.

    Mouse and rat control

  • Inside and outside walls

    Spiders and other insects collect around windows, door thresholds and the base of the outside walls.

    Insect and spider control

Damage repair

We fix what they broke

Animals leave behind soiled insulation, chewed vents and open gaps. Repair is part of every wildlife job.

  • Insulation replacement. Flattened, soiled attic insulation taken out and replaced.
  • Entry points sealed. The holes and gaps they used are closed off.
  • Follow-up under guarantee. Hear something again during the guarantee? We come back and check.

How it works

From the first call to sealed up

  1. You call

    Tell us what you’re hearing or seeing, where in the house and at what time of day.

  2. We inspect

    The outside of the house, the roofline and the attic, to find what’s there and how it gets in.

  3. We remove

    The animals or insects come out, along with the nest or den.

  4. We repair

    Damaged insulation is replaced and entry points are closed.

  5. We stand behind it

    If they come back during the guarantee period, call and we’ll return.
