Animal damage repair in Kitchener

Animals left damage in your attic or roofline? We replace soiled insulation and seal entry points so they can’t get back in.

Animals leave more behind than noise. Attic insulation gets flattened and soiled, vents and soffits get chewed or pulled up, and every opening they used stays open for the next animal.

We repair that damage as part of the job and seal the entry points, so the problem doesn’t repeat.

Signs you have damage repair

  • Flattened, stained or damp-looking insulation in the attic
  • Chewed or pulled-up roof vents and soffits
  • Torn screens and gaps around the roofline
  • A lingering smell in the attic after the animals are gone
  • Daylight showing through an opening in the roofline

How we handle it

  1. AssessWe check the insulation, vents, soffits and roofline for damage.
  2. ReplaceSoiled or flattened insulation is removed and replaced.
  3. RepairChewed or damaged vents, screens and soffits are repaired.
  4. SealEvery entry point we find is closed off.

Why you shouldn’t leave the damage

Once the animals are gone, it is tempting to treat the problem as solved. The attic tells a different story. Insulation that has been flattened, soiled or soaked loses much of its ability to hold heat, and the odour left behind can attract the next animal. An unrepaired opening is an invitation to the next raccoon, squirrel or bat, and a gap in the roofline also lets water in.

What repair usually covers

The scope depends on what the animal did, and we explain it after the inspection. It commonly includes:

  • Removing soiled, matted or contaminated insulation and replacing it.
  • Repairing or replacing chewed vents, screens, soffits and fascia.
  • Closing every opening on the roofline, chimney and foundation that we found.
  • Checking for chewed wiring and telling you if an electrician should look at it.

Why this belongs with the removal

Repairing the damage as part of the same job means the person who found how the animals got in is the person who closes it. That is how we stand behind the work. If animals return during the guarantee period, call and we come back to check. Ask us about the guarantee terms when you book.

Questions about damage repair

Is damage repair included with wildlife removal?

Yes. Replacing soiled attic insulation and sealing the openings is part of our wildlife jobs.

Why replace the insulation?

Animals flatten it and soil it with droppings and urine, so it no longer does its job and can leave a smell behind.

What happens if the opening isn’t sealed?

It can be reused by the next animal, which is why we seal every entry point we find.

Do I need to replace all the insulation?

Not always. We check the attic and replace the insulation that is soiled, flattened or wet.

Can you repair the roofline too?

Chewed or damaged vents, screens and soffits are part of repair. Tell us what you can see and we will check on the visit.

How do I know the repair worked?

We close every opening we find and stand behind the work. If you hear animals again during the guarantee period, call and we come back.
