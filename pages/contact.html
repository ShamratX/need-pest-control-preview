FAQ

Before you call

Which animals and pests do you deal with?

Raccoons, squirrels, skunks, bats, rats and mice, plus wasp and bee nests. We don’t handle general insects or spiders.

Do you repair the damage animals leave behind?

Yes. We replace damaged attic insulation and seal the entry points the animals used, so the next one can’t get in the same way.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. If you hear or see signs of animals again during the guarantee period, call and we’ll come back to check. Ask us for the guarantee details when you book.

What area do you serve?

We’re based in Kitchener and work on Kitchener homes. If you’re nearby, call and we’ll tell you whether we can get to you.

How can I tell what’s in my attic?

Timing helps. Scratching early in the morning usually points to squirrels. Heavier thumping at night is more often raccoons. Tell us when you hear it and we’ll know what to look for. Our attic noises guide has a quick comparison.
