Skunk removal in Kitchener

Skunk living under your deck, porch or shed? We remove skunks and close off the space underneath so another can’t move in.

Skunks dig dens under decks, porches, sheds and steps. Most people notice the smell before they ever see one.

We remove the skunks and close off the space underneath, so the den can’t be reused.

Signs you have skunks

  • A musky smell near the deck, porch or shed
  • A freshly dug hole or burrow under a structure
  • Small cone-shaped holes dug in the lawn
  • A pet that came back sprayed
  • Activity at dusk and through the night

How we handle it

  1. InspectWe locate the den and check for other openings under the structure.
  2. RemoveThe skunks are removed from under the deck, porch or shed.
  3. Close offThe space underneath is closed so another skunk can’t move in.

Why skunks dig under decks, porches and sheds

Skunks look for a dry, covered space close to food. The gap under a deck, porch, shed or set of steps is ideal: it is sheltered, hard for predators to reach and often dug out for them by other animals first. They also dig small cone-shaped holes in lawns while hunting grubs, which is often the first sign of a skunk in the yard.

Don’t seal the den until you know it’s empty

Skunks have their young in late spring, and the young stay in the den for weeks. Closing the entrance with a mother or kits still inside traps them, and a frightened skunk is more likely to spray. We check the den first, remove the skunks and only then close off the space.

What to do if a skunk has sprayed

If a pet has been sprayed, keep the animal outside and out of upholstery. A mix of hydrogen peroxide, baking soda and dish soap is a common home remedy, but check with your vet before using it, especially near the eyes. Skunks can carry rabies, so if you or a pet has been bitten or scratched, contact your doctor or vet and your local public health unit.

Make the space unattractive afterwards

After the skunk is gone, the area under the structure still looks like a den. A lasting fix closes the gap with a barrier that is buried below the surface so skunks can’t dig under it. To keep them from returning, also:

  • Bring pet food indoors and keep garbage and green-bin lids closed.
  • Pick up fallen fruit and spilled bird seed.
  • Keep the lawn free of grubs if you notice the cone-shaped holes.

Questions about skunks

When are skunks most active?

At dusk and through the night. In summer they raise their young under decks and sheds.

Why is my lawn full of small holes?

Skunks dig for grubs and insects in the lawn, leaving small cone-shaped holes.

Will the skunk spray when you remove it?

Skunks spray when frightened. We work calmly and carefully to keep that unlikely, but we can’t promise it won’t happen.

How long do skunks stay under a deck?

It varies. A skunk may use the den for a short stay or all season. If a mother has young, they stay longer.

How do I keep skunks from coming back?

Closing the gap under the structure with a barrier buried below the surface is the main step. Removing pet food and loose garbage helps too.
